
=== Procedure: Produccion.TotalPaletPrensado ===

--- Source (Source) ---
        
        &TotalPalets = 0
	
	for each DB.Palet
		where PaletId > 0
		where PaletPrensadoFinId = &PrensadoId
		where PaletEstatus in (EstatusPalet.Terminado, EstatusPalet.Embarcado)
		&TotalPalets += 1	
	endfor

--- Rules (Rules) ---
parm(in:&PrensadoId, out:&TotalPalets);
