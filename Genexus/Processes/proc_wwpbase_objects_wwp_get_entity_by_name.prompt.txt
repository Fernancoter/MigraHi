
=== Procedure: WWPBaseObjects.WWP_GetEntityByName ===

--- Source (Source) ---
For Each WWP_Entity
	Where WWPEntityName = &WWPEntityName
	&WWPEntityId = WWPEntityId
	Exit
When None
	&WWP_Entity = New()
	&WWP_Entity.DB.WWPEntityName = &WWPEntityName
	&WWP_Entity.Save()
	If &WWP_Entity.Success()
		&WWPEntityId = &WWP_Entity.WWPEntityId
	EndIf
EndFor

--- Rules (Rules) ---
Parm(in:&WWPEntityName, out:&WWPEntityId);
