### Grid1PaginationBar.ChangePage (Event)

```genexus
/* Generated by DVelop Work With Plus Pattern [Start] - Do not change */
	Do Case
		Case Grid1PaginationBar.SelectedPage = !'Previous'
			&Grid1CurrentPage -= 1
			Grid1.PreviousPage()
		Case Grid1PaginationBar.SelectedPage = !'Next'
			&Grid1CurrentPage += 1
			Grid1.NextPage()
		Otherwise
			&PageToGo.FromString(Grid1PaginationBar.SelectedPage)
			&Grid1CurrentPage = &PageToGo
			Grid1.GotoPage(&PageToGo)
	EndCase
	Refresh
	/* Generated by DVelop Work With Plus Pattern [End] - Do not change */
```
